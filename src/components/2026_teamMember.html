Meet the Team

Julie Cancio Harper (she/her)

Julie Cancio Harper has been a creative project manager for most of her career. First in the publishing industry, then in film production, she focused on finding the three-way win — for her clients, for their audiences, and for her co-workers. In 2025, Julie served as the manager for Lit Youngstown’s Fall Literary Festival. She is a reiki master teacher and tarot reader hosting workshops at The Dreaming Tree in Hubbard, Ohio. Julie is also the executive producer of the Neighborhood Bookstore author interview podcast. She earned a Bachelor of Arts in English literature from Kenyon College and attended the University of Denver Publishing Institute.

Craig Duster (he/him)

Craig Duster is a Sagittarius and enjoys long walks on the beach. He is also the Vice President of Lit Youngstown and the owner and Head Ne'er-Do-Well of the POP! Art Books Culture, Youngstown's 2023, 2024 & 2025 Readers' Choice award-winning used bookstore. Before founding POP! with his wife Christine, Craig was the Special Project Coordinator for Sally Centigrade Gallery and has curated gallery shows in Colorado and Ohio. Craig holds a bachelor's degree from the University of Colorado.
