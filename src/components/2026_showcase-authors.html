Cori Wamsley

Cori Wamsley writes sweet, closed door romances filled with heart, humor, and the kind of emotional clarity that sneaks up on you in the best way. Her books explore identity, healing, and the courage it takes to begin again—all wrapped in character driven stories where love grows in peaceful moments, friendships matter, and hope always gets the final word. Readers often tell her that her books feel like a deep breath, a warm hug, and a belly laugh, which she considers the highest compliment.

She’s the author of Ashes and Other Inheritances, Exactly What It Looks Like, and several earlier titles. When she’s not writing, Cori works as a publishing consultant, writing coach, and editor through her company, Aurora Corialis Publishing, helping authors build stories with emotional resonance and careers with staying power.

Cori lives in Pennsylvania with her family, where she can usually be found drafting new chapters, cheering on her kids’ creative chaos, or hunting down allergy friendly chocolate like it’s a competitive sport. Fun facts: she’s in a long-term relationship with her TBR and believes every good romance starts with one honest moment. She loves connecting with readers who crave heartfelt stories, soft humor, and second chances.

Kara M. Zone

Kara M. Zone is an award-winning, best-selling indie author who crafts tales of magic, mayhem, and mischief. Kara provides creative writing & indie publishing workshops to help writers at any stage create the best stories for their voices, ideas, and brands. Her true passion is helping others reach their goals, no matter what publishing path they're interested in.
