Basket Raffles

Full raffle baskets and individual raffle basket items can be brought with your vending supplies on the morning of the event (November 7, 2026).

Basket raffle tickets can be purchased by attendees throughout the day of the event until the final announcement. We will be selling tickets for $1 each or $5 for 6 tickets. Winners will be announced at 11AM, 1PM, and, finally, at 3PM. Depending on the number of baskets, there may be multiple winners at each announcement. Attendees must be present to win.

All profits from the basket raffles will be donated to local literary nonprofit, Lit Youngstown.
