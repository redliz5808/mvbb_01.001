After Happy Hour
(with Last-Picked Books)

After Happy Hour was started in 2014 by a workshop group that met in Pittsburgh's Bloomfield neighborhood, and though the editorial team has evolved over the years, it remains an independent publisher of work in any and all genres. It publishes three issues a year: two online issues and one print issue containing work from the annual contest.

Books a Go Go

My name is Cathy Klein and I own Books a Go Go. I have a store in downtown Ravenna and celebrating my first year anniversary here, but I have had my shop as a pop up since 2022. I love connecting with new readers and would love to participate if possible.

I carry a little bit of all genres and also cute bookish merchandise primarily sourced from local artisans. My shop features local author events, book clubs, a writing group and more. I focus on new release titles, but also have standards everyone loves.
